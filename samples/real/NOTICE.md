# Real-world test documents

These are real, publicly published contract texts, used to test the checker on documents its author did not write.
They are included under their original open licences. Each file is the published text with HTML tags removed and
Markdown links reduced to their link text (nothing else changed).

These are templates or public terms, not advice, and the checker's findings on them say nothing about the quality of the originals.

`samples/adversarial/` holds a synthetic contract written by the author to contain common phrasings the first review found the tool missing.
It is labelled as synthetic in the answer key and is not counted as independent evidence.
